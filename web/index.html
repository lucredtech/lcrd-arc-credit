Unsecured USDC credit, priced on-chain from a credit score.

LCRD scores a borrower's real financial activity. The contract turns that score into a tier, an APR and a credit limit with a published formula anyone can check, and records every repayment so good borrowers pay less and can borrow more over time. Only a hash of the score report is on-chain, never personal data.

Get a credit line

No forms. LCRD scores your Stellar wallet's on-chain history and, if you qualify, opens a line for this Arc wallet automatically. You sign one message with each wallet to prove both are yours; nothing moves between chains and no personal data goes on-chain. Automatic lines are capped at 1 USDC while the pool is small.

  1. Connect this Arc wallet (top right).
  2. Have Freighter installed with the Stellar wallet you use for DeFi.
  3. Sign two messages: one in your Arc wallet, one in Freighter.

Underwriter

Visible only to the underwriter wallet. Enter the borrower's LCRD score; the contract sets the tier, APR and maximum line. The score reference is the keccak-256 hash of the score report (leave it empty to use a placeholder hash for testing).

How LCRD scores and prices credit

1 · The score (0–100), off-chain

LCRD reads a borrower's real financial behaviour: completed borrow-and-repay cycles where they exist, otherwise activity (protocols used, volume, active days, length of track record) plus income signals. The report is hashed; only the hash goes on-chain, so no personal data does.

Tier A 67–100 · Tier B 34–66 · Tier C 0–33

2 · The price, on-chain

The contract computes the APR from the score with LCRD's published policy; nobody can quote a borrower a different rate.

APR = 8% cost of funds
+ score premium: 20% at score 0 → 3% at score 100
+ 3% unsecured surcharge
− 0.5% per completed cycle (up to 2%)
kept between 6% and 36%

Example: score 71 → 8% + 7.93% + 3% = 18.93%. Check any score with quote(score, cycles).

3 · Repayments build credit

Every repayment is recorded on-chain. A cycle completes when a loan using at least 25% of the line, held at least 7 days, is repaid in full. Each completed cycle cuts the APR by 0.5% automatically and lets the line grow by another starter amount.

Starter line: A 100 USDC · B 75 USDC · C none
Ceiling: A 2,500 USDC · B 250 USDC
A write-off resets completed cycles to zero

Credit lines
