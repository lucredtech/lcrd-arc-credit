LCRD Credit Line Arc mainnet

Credit lines in USDC, underwritten by a credit score.

Lenders pool USDC. LCRD scores a borrower's real financial activity off-chain and opens a line with a limit, an APR and a risk tier. Only a hash of the score report goes on-chain, never personal data. Borrowers draw and repay in USDC, and repaid interest raises every lender's share.
