Get a credit line: two ways to qualify

1 Provide liquidity secured · no score needed

Deposit USDC in the pool, then borrow up to 71.25% of it. Your deposit keeps earning; the part backing your loan is locked until you repay. 15% APR with no score, cheaper with every completed repayment. Best if you have no on-chain history yet: it builds one.

2 Use your Stellar wallet unsecured · score 55+

LCRD scores your Stellar DeFi history. Tier A, or a score of 55+ in tier B, gets an unsecured line opened automatically (capped at 1 USDC while the pool is small). Sign one message with this Arc wallet and one with Freighter to prove both are yours; nothing moves between chains.

How LCRD scores and prices credit

Step 1 · off-chain

The score (0–100)

LCRD reads a borrower's real financial behaviour: completed borrow-and-repay cycles where they exist, otherwise activity (protocols used, volume, active days, length of track record) plus income signals. The report is hashed; only the hash goes on-chain, so no personal data does.

Tier A 67–100 · Tier B 34–66 · Tier C 0–33
Unsecured: tier A, or tier B with 55+
Secured (against your deposit): no score needed

Step 2 · on-chain

The price

The contract computes the APR from the score with LCRD's published policy; nobody can quote a borrower a different rate.

Unsecured APR = 8% cost of funds
+ score premium: 20% at score 0 → 3% at 100
+ 3% unsecured surcharge
Secured APR = 8% + 35% of the score premium
(15% with no score)
Both: − 0.5% per completed cycle (up to 2%),
kept between 6% and 36%

Example: score 71 → 8% + 7.93% + 3% = 18.93%. Check any score with quote(score, cycles).

Step 3 · on-chain

Repayments build credit

Every repayment is recorded on-chain. A cycle completes when a loan using at least 25% of the line, held at least 7 days, is repaid in full. Each completed cycle cuts the APR by 0.5% automatically and lets the line grow by another starter amount.

Unsecured starter: A 100 USDC · B (55+) 75 USDC
Ceiling: A 2,500 USDC · B 250 USDC
Secured: up to 71.25% of your deposit
A default resets completed cycles to zero
